# Transaction-only analytics: core commit

This first implementation commit adds aggregate calculations and regression tests. The enhanced dashboard entry point and rendering are a separate pending commit; no existing application behavior is changed yet. Do not claim the complete dashboard is deployed.

Core APIs: segment_summary (full-population median/IQR, finite/missing counts, 90/365-day exposure and normalized shares); concentration (all selected customers including zero spend, 90-day top fractions and at most 101 exact curve points); monthly_summary (non-overlapping months from existing basket outputs, fixed cutoff population and purchasing components); category_summary (365-day category-history participation/spend share/recurrence without pairs); cohort_summary (first-observed later-shopping-day repeats within 30/60/90 days, mature follow-up, capture gates and 95% Wilson intervals); transition_summary (matched label rates with origin/destination/unmatched reconciliation).

Definitions: 90-day bounds are cutoff minus 89 through cutoff; 365-day bounds minus 364, inclusive. Basket outputs already use customer + transaction + date. Zero spend yields undefined proportions. Top fractions round up to whole customers; ties may split. IQR is within-group variation, not confidence. Minimum history changes denominators; rule-defining differences are not independent validation. Monthly buyers are not additive unique people; fixed populations include customers first observed after earlier months. Category participation overlaps; all selected customers, including inactive, remain in its denominator. Category spend shares include all categories; recurrence is at least three in-window shopping days. Product established share is a subset of repeat share, not a separate stacked part. HHI depends on category breadth.

Repeat cohorts use first observation, not verified acquisition. Same-day baskets are excluded from returns. Eligible follow-up ends at the selected cutoff; incomplete horizons and unconfirmed capture suppress rates. Denominators vary by horizon. Wilson intervals assume independent customers and are not multiplicity-adjusted. Origin transitions are unfiltered, destination filtered: unmatched customers are not churn and destination-only customers are not acquisitions.

No product matrix, full-catalog market basket mining, pair expansion, automatic seasonality adjustment, calibrated churn or anomaly alerts. Currency unspecified; amounts are not profit or net revenue after refunds. Capture is user-confirmed, not independently verified. Source history/tenure do not verify acquisition; duplicates remain preserved. No production dataset or commercial findings claimed.

Run `python -m pip install -r requirements.txt` then `python -m unittest discover -v`. Polars/Streamlit were unavailable during authoring. Definition checks passed; Polars tests skipped. CI rejects skipped tests. Before merge, require full regressions without skips, representative-data reconciliation, runtime/UI validation after the UI commit, accessibility checks and scale benchmarks. Full-population sorting and joins remain potentially expensive; bounded charts do not eliminate server cost.
